HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to a useful preview

  1. Choose a tile.Your rear camera opens after you grant permission.
  2. Point toward the floor.SegFormer labels floor pixels and leaves furniture, rugs, walls, people, and objects visible.
  3. Freeze for the best result.A still frame gets a steadier mask. Refine lets you paint or erase any difficult boundary.
  4. Adjust only if needed.Tile size, floor depth, tilt, vanishing point, grout, rotation, and blend can be corrected before saving.

This is a visualization, not a color-calibrated material sample or a measuring instrument.
